# Cancellation and Refund Policy (v2 — CURRENT)

Effective 2026-08-10, customers may cancel their Nimbus Telecom service
within 14 days of the start of a billing period for a full refund of that
period's plan fee, provided the account has no outstanding overage
charges. This shortens the previous 30-day window to align with updated
regional consumer-protection guidance. Cancellations requested after the
14-day window are not eligible for a refund of the current period's plan
fee, though any unused prepaid balance is still credited to the
customer's final bill. Refund requests must be submitted through the
support channel and are processed within 5 business days of approval.
All customer-facing channels, including the AI support copilot, must
reflect this 14-day window as of the effective date above.
