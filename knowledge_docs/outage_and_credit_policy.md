# Service Outage Credit Policy

Customers affected by an unplanned network outage lasting longer than 4
continuous hours are eligible for a pro-rated service credit for the
affected period, up to a maximum of $50 per incident. Credits for outage
impact must always be confirmed by a human support agent before being
applied to an account; the support copilot may propose a credit but must
never apply one automatically. Planned maintenance outages, communicated
at least 48 hours in advance, are not eligible for outage credits.
