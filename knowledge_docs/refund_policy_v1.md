# Cancellation and Refund Policy (v1 — SUPERSEDED)

Customers may cancel their Nimbus Telecom service within 30 days of the
start of a billing period for a full refund of that period's plan fee,
provided the account has no outstanding overage charges. Cancellations
requested after the 30-day window are not eligible for a refund of the
current period's plan fee, though any unused prepaid balance is credited
to the customer's final bill. Refund requests must be submitted through
the support channel and are processed within 5 business days of approval.
